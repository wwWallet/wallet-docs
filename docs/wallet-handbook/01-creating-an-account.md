# Creating an Account

To create a wwWallet account, you need a [supported browser and operating system](../getting-started/01-browser-os-compatibility.md), as well as a device or security key that can store a passkey.

## Steps to Create Your Account

1. Open your wwWallet app or visit [demo.wwwallet.org](https://demo.wwwallet.org) in your browser.

2. On the welcome screen, select **Sign up**.

3. Enter a name in the **Wallet Name** field. This name helps you identify the account when you sign in.

4. Choose a passkey option and follow your browser or device prompts. Depending on your device and browser, your passkey may be stored in a password manager such as Google Password Manager or iCloud Keychain, on the device itself, on a nearby device or on an external security key such as a YubiKey.

5. When wwWallet asks you to authenticate once more, select **Continue** and use the same passkey you just created. Once setup is complete, wwWallet opens your new account.

> **Important**: Your passkey protects access to your wallet. Keep it available and consider [adding a secondary passkey](./02-adding-a-secondary-passkey.md) after creating your account.
